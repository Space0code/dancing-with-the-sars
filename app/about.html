About Parallax

Parallax uses radar satellites to find where the land surface has changed, and gives each change a plain-language label from public records: expected, needs review, or high concern.

Where it comes from

Built at the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 in Barcelona, for the challenge “Dancing with the SARs”.

Data and credits

Satellite data comes from NASA, ISRO and ESA, and the records behind the labels from each place's own authorities. Details are on How it works. The code is open in the project repository.
