# What past Space Apps winners did in their demos

Lessons for our deck from the 2025 Global Winners, specific to Space Apps. Last updated 2026-11-15.

**Basis:** the demos of the 10 2025 Global Winners (decks read in full: Twisters, GaiaLEO), the judges' write-ups on
[Meet the 2025 Global Winners](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2025/meet-the-2025-global-winners/), and five
2025 Global Finalists that did not win: Celesta, Ceylon Xzora, HUEI-YING-OH and iNFINITY MISSION (all in
Twisters' challenge, "Will It Rain On My Parade?") and CloudMap (Earth observation, air quality). Decks read in full:
Celesta, Ceylon Xzora, CloudMap.
**Limits:** small sample (2 winning vs 3 losing decks); judges score the whole project page, not only the deck. The
2025 SAR-challenge team (FarmHubGlobal, Honorable Mention) and two other Earth-observation honorable mentions keep
their project private, so they could not be compared.

## The 10 global awards: each is won in one category
Best Use of Science · Best Use of Data · Best Use of Technology · Galactic Impact · Best Mission Concept · Most
Inspirational · Best Use of Storytelling · Global Connection · Art & Technology · Local Impact.
A winner doesn't have to beat everyone; it has to be the obvious pick for **one** category.
**For us:** aim at **Best Use of Data** (NASA data is the core: OPERA, HLS, NISAR) and keep **Local Impact** in
reach (one real case, Muhos). **Show it, don't say it:** HUEI-YING-OH wrote "We aim at the Best Use of Data award"
in its summary and didn't win.

## What the winners did
- **One plain human sentence before any tech.** Every winner's write-up opens with who benefits, not the method:
  Twisters, "Will it rain on my parade?"; Photonics Odyssey, a child studying by lantern in Ladakh. **Ours:** the
  regulator on Monday morning, and Muhos.
- **Title = the challenge's own hook.** Twisters' title slide is their challenge's question in huge letters, with
  team name and all members underneath. **Ours:** "Dancing with the SARs" on the title slide next to Parallax.
- **Short decks.** Both winning PDFs are 6–7 slides with few words. Twisters' deck has a single screenshot of the
  real app, with the caption "It's better if we show you". Neither deck has a methods wall.
- **Problem told as a user story.** Twisters shows the problem as a chat between two users ("It predicted rain
  between 3 and 5 p.m., so we rescheduled"). Concrete scene, not a statistic.
- **Numbers with sources on the slide.** GaiaLEO footnotes every market figure (McKinsey, Net Zero Insights) and
  ends with concrete outcome numbers (52 orbital + 42 ground sites). **Ours:** the Muhos hectares and dates with
  their sources.
- **Compliance is visible.** GaiaLEO writes "Logo made with AI" right on the title slide. Team roles are shown
  (Data Engineer, Data Scientist…).
- **A live, public product link.** 9 of 10 winners link a working site (Vercel, Netlify, Framer, own domain);
  Twisters ends on a QR code to it. **Ours:** end on a QR to the Pages app.
- **Format didn't matter.** 3 of the 10 winning demos are decks (Twisters, GaiaLEO, Photonics Odyssey), 7 are
  ≤ 30 s videos. A deck is no handicap.

## Same challenge, same product: what separated the winner from four finalists
In "Will It Rain On My Parade?" Twisters (won) and Celesta, Ceylon Xzora, HUEI-YING-OH, iNFINITY MISSION (finalists)
all built nearly the same thing: the chance of "very hot / very wet" weather for a chosen place and date, mostly
from NASA POWER. Celesta even used the same title. So the tech didn't decide it; the telling did. The losing decks:
- **Paragraph slides.** Celesta's problem slide is ~110 words, Ceylon Xzora's "What is" and "Methodology" slides are
  full paragraphs. Twisters' busiest slide is ~40 words.
- **A tech-stack slide.** Celesta (Next.js, Tailwind, Recharts…), CloudMap (React, Flask, TensorFlow, PyTorch…) and
  Ceylon Xzora all list frameworks. No winning deck does.
- **The demo as a URL, not a picture.** Celesta's "LIVE DEMO" slide and CloudMap's deck show only a link. Twisters
  shows the app screen.
- **Impact spread thin.** Ceylon Xzora lists events, agriculture, tourism, construction, energy and "saves lives".
  Twisters shows one person rescheduling one event.
- **Template leftovers.** Celesta's footer still says "20XX", and its photos (forests, pine needles) have nothing to
  do with the app.

## Other things that didn't carry a team to a win
- **NASA data as decoration.** CloudMap's NASA sources are all marked "(Ready for Integration)"; the real data came
  from OpenAQ and WAQI. Celesta says "NASA & Open Weather APIs". Both reached the finalists and stopped there.
  **Ours:** name the NASA products on a slide and show that the pictures and numbers come from them.
- **Numbers without a source.** CloudMap claims "94% accuracy" with nothing behind it; GaiaLEO (won) cites every
  figure.
- **Mixed-up form fields.** iNFINITY MISSION put its website in "Demo" and a Drive file in "Final Project". **Ours:**
  deck in Demo, Pages app in Final Project.
- **Borderline length.** HUEI-YING-OH's video is 31 s on YouTube (limit 30 s). Not our problem with a deck, but
  any clip we embed should stay ≤ 10 s.
- **Polish alone.** Sphyrna (Global Finalist) had a 3D-animated video judges called "exceptionally polished", per its
  own [write-up](https://contra.com/p/rOUL6eZM-sphyrna-nasa-space-apps-2025). It reached the finalists, not a win.
- **Dead links.** One 2025 winner's Drive demo link is broken a year later. Host the deck where it stays: repo or
  Release PDF plus Google Slides.
