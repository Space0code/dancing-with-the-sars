# What past Space Apps winners did in their demos

Lessons for our deck from the 2025 Global Winners, specific to Space Apps. Last updated 2026-11-15.

**Basis:** the demos of the 10 2025 Global Winners (decks read in full: Twisters, GaiaLEO), the judges' write-ups on
[Meet the 2025 Global Winners](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2025/meet-the-2025-global-winners/), and one
non-winning Global Finalist (Sphyrna, self-reported on [Contra](https://contra.com/p/rOUL6eZM-sphyrna-nasa-space-apps-2025)).
**Limit:** the finalist and honorable-mention lists only render in a browser, so the "didn't win" side is n = 1. Treat
those points as weak evidence.

## The 10 global awards: each is won in one category
Best Use of Science · Best Use of Data · Best Use of Technology · Galactic Impact · Best Mission Concept · Most
Inspirational · Best Use of Storytelling · Global Connection · Art & Technology · Local Impact.
A winner doesn't have to beat everyone; it has to be the obvious pick for **one** category.
**For us:** pitch for **Best Use of Data** (NASA data is the core: OPERA, HLS, NISAR) and keep **Local Impact** in
reach (one real case, Muhos). The deck should make that category easy to see.

## What the winners did
- **One plain human sentence before any tech.** Every winner's write-up opens with who benefits, not the method:
  Twisters, "Will it rain on my parade?"; Photonics Odyssey, a child studying by lantern in Ladakh. **Ours:** the
  regulator on Monday morning, and Muhos.
- **Title = the challenge's own hook.** Twisters' title slide is their challenge's question in huge letters, with
  team name and all members underneath. **Ours:** "Dancing with the SARs" on the title slide next to Parallax.
- **Short decks.** Both winning PDFs are 6–7 slides with few words. Twisters' deck has a single screenshot of the
  real app, with the caption "It's better if we show you". Neither deck has a methods wall.
- **Problem told as a user story.** Twisters shows the problem as a chat between two users ("It predicted rain
  between 3 and 5 p.m., so we rescheduled"). Concrete scene, not a statistic.
- **Numbers with sources on the slide.** GaiaLEO footnotes every market figure (McKinsey, Net Zero Insights) and
  ends with concrete outcome numbers (52 orbital + 42 ground sites). **Ours:** the Muhos hectares and dates with
  their sources.
- **Compliance is visible.** GaiaLEO writes "Logo made with AI" right on the title slide. Team roles are shown
  (Data Engineer, Data Scientist…).
- **A live, public product link.** 9 of 10 winners link a working site (Vercel, Netlify, Framer, own domain);
  Twisters ends on a QR code to it. **Ours:** end on a QR to the Pages app.
- **Format didn't matter.** 3 of the 10 winning demos are decks (Twisters, GaiaLEO, Photonics Odyssey), 7 are
  ≤ 30 s videos. A deck is no handicap.

## What didn't carry a team to a win
- **Polish alone.** Sphyrna (Global Finalist, not a winner) had a 3D-animated video the judges called "exceptionally
  polished". It got them to the finalists, not to a win. (n = 1)
- **Dead links.** One 2025 winner's Drive demo link is broken a year later. Host the deck where it stays: repo or
  Release PDF plus Google Slides.
